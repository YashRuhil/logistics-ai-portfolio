# 🚚 AI-Assisted Logistics Customer Service 📦

### 🌟 Project Overview

**Project:** AI-Assisted Logistics Customer Service

**🎯 Business Problem:**
Communicating delayed shipment information accurately and clearly to both customers and internal coordination teams.

**🛠️ Tools Used:**

* 🤖 ChatGPT – AI-assisted content generation and prompt testing
* 🐙 GitHub – Project documentation and storage
* 📝 Microsoft Word – Report preparation
* 📄 PDF – Final report format

**🧠 Strategies Practised:**

* 🎯 **Zero-Shot Prompting** – Generating responses using direct instructions.
* 🧩 **Context and Constraints** – Providing relevant details, word limits, and requirements.
* 🎭 **Role Prompting** – Assigning specific roles to guide AI responses.
* 📝 **Few-Shot Prompting** – Using examples to guide the output format and style.
* 📊 **Structured Output** – Organising information into tables and clearly labelled sections.
* 👥 **Audience-Based Prompting** – Adapting tone and detail for customers and internal teams.

**📂 Outputs Created:**

* 📧 Customer email
* ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) responses
* 📊 Structured support table
* 📱 Customer SMS notification
* 🏭 Internal warehouse coordination briefing

**🙋 My Contribution:**
I designed and refined prompts, checked shipment details, corrected outdated delivery and update timings, applied word limits, and verified consistency across all outputs. I also adapted the tone and level of detail to meet the needs of customers and internal coordination teams.

**💡 Key Learning:**
I learned that clear instructions, relevant context, and appropriate constraints improve AI-generated responses. I also understood the importance of refining prompts, tailoring outputs to different audiences, and verifying information before using AI-generated content.

**⚠️ Limitations:**
This project is a classroom simulation based on fictional information. It is not connected to live shipment tracking systems and cannot independently verify real-time shipment status or delivery estimates. All AI-generated outputs require human review before real-world use.

---

✨ **Key Takeaway:** Better prompts + human verification = clearer and more reliable logistics communication. 🚚🤖
